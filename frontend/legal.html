Limitations

  • The model misses guns in unusual poses or when they are small or hidden.
  • The model gives false alarms on lookalike objects.
  • Multi-frame confirmation removes flicker but not a steady lookalike.
  • Notification to authorized personnel is not implemented; the alert log is the only record.

Privacy notice

Image upload mode

Uploaded images are processed in memory and are not stored on the server. No copy of the uploaded file is written to disk. The image is decoded, passed to the model, and the annotated result is returned as a base64-encoded JPEG in the API response.

Live camera mode

In camera mode, the video stream stays on your computer. Frames are captured locally, scaled, encoded as JPEG, and sent to the local server (http://127.0.0.1:8000) for inference. No video data leaves your machine.

Alert evidence

An evidence image is saved to the server's alerts/images/ directory only when an alert is raised (either by the multi-frame confirmation in the browser or by the alert_video.py script). The evidence image is saved alongside a row in alerts/alert_log.csv with the same columns: timestamp, source, frame, video_time_s, max_conf, evidence_image.

No tracking

  • No cookies are set.
  • No analytics or telemetry are collected.
  • No external network requests are made by the frontend or the API.
  • All processing happens locally on your machine or your local network.

Data retention

Alert logs and evidence images persist on the local filesystem until you delete them. You can remove the alerts/ directory at any time.
